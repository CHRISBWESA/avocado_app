How to Use

1

Capture or Upload

Take a photo of your avocado using the camera, or choose one from your gallery. Make sure the avocado is clearly visible and well-lit.

💡 Tip: Hold the avocado against a plain background for best results
2

Tap Analyze

Once your photo is uploaded, tap the "Analyze" button to send the image to our AI model for ripeness detection.

⏳ The analysis takes just a few seconds
3

Get Your Result

The app will display whether your avocado is RIPE or UNRIPE, along with a confidence percentage. A green badge means it's ready to eat!

🥑 RIPE = ready to eat | UNRIPE = wait a few more days
4

Try Again

Not happy with the result? Tap "Reset" to take a new photo and try again. You can analyze as many avocados as you want.
